Breakfast Inclusive Rate

Best Rate Guaranteed

1. Eligibility & Booking

The Breakfast Inclusive Rate is available to all guests booking directly through the Kimadi Stays website, the Kimadi Reservations Centre, or at the property. It is not available via third-party online travel agents or wholesale channels, unless stated otherwise in a specific partnership agreement.

2. Inclusions

  • Daily breakfast for the number of registered guests in the room (maximum two adults per room under this rate unless specifically agreed).
  • Breakfast is served in the designated dining venue of each property during published breakfast hours, typically 07:00–10:30 local time.
  • In-room or poolside breakfast service may be available at participating properties and may attract a nominal surcharge; please enquire at the time of booking.
  • Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is provided in-room and across public areas throughout the duration of the stay.
  • Member discount of up to 15% applies to Kimadi Members only, is calculated on the Best Available Rate for the same dates and room type, and cannot be combined with any other promotional discount.

3. Cancellation Policy

Reservations under the Breakfast Inclusive Rate may be modified or cancelled without charge up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled date of arrival, local property time. Cancellations received within 48 hours of arrival will attract a one-night room-and-breakfast charge. No-shows will be charged the full booking value. Special event dates, peak season periods, and long-stay reservations may be subject to separate cancellation terms as communicated at the time of booking.

4. Pricing & Currency

All rates displayed are in Indian Rupees (INR), inclusive of applicable GST unless specified otherwise. Rates are per room per night and are subject to change without prior notice. Kimadi Stays reserves the right to adjust pricing due to changes in government levies, seasonal demand, or operational factors.

5. Property-Specific Conditions

Certain Kimadi properties may apply additional specific conditions to this offer, which will be communicated clearly on the respective property booking page. Kimadi Stays is not responsible for any misrepresentation of offer details on unauthorised third-party websites.

6. General

  • This offer is subject to availability and may be withdrawn or amended at any time without prior notice.
  • Kimadi Stays reserves the right to substitute breakfast components based on seasonality and local availability, without compromising quality.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India and are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New Delhi.
